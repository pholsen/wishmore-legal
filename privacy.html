Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

WishMore is a private, internal tool used only by its developers to view statistics of their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

  • Basic profile information (display name, open ID) of the authorizing account
  • Account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts)
  • The list of the account's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts

How we use it

The data is used only to show the account owner how their own videos perform. It is stored locally on the owner's computer, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local data folder removes all stored data.

Contact

For questions, open an issue at github.com/pholsen/wishmore-legal.
